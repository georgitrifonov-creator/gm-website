Frequently asked questions

What clients ask us beforehand

01Where does the bootcamp take place?

On site at our headquarters in Bulgaria. Your team spends five days there working with our consultants, with real conversations and real clients.

02Does the whole team take part or just individuals?

It delivers the most when the entire sales and key account team takes part, because the processes and competition are then the same for everyone. We agree the group size with you in advance.

03How is it different from ongoing support?

Ongoing support builds structures over several months. The bootcamp condenses this into one intensive week and gives your team a tangible boost. The two combine well.

04What does the bootcamp cost?

That depends on team size and focus areas. Send us a no-obligation enquiry about the bootcamp and we will get back to you with a suitable proposal.
