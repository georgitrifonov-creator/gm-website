Privacy policy

Last updated: November 2026

1. At a glance

This website uses no cookies for advertising or analytics purposes and does not include any tracking services. Fonts and images are hosted on our own server. We only process personal data when you write to us, book an appointment or apply for a job.

2. Controller

GM SERVICES Ltd., ul. Polkovnik Lukashov 5, 3000 Vratsa, Bulgaria
Email: info@gmsrvcs.com, phone: +359 8777 133 04

3. Hosting and server logs

The website is hosted by Vercel Inc. (USA) and delivered via its European network. When you visit the site, technically necessary data is processed (IP address, time, page accessed, browser). This is necessary to deliver the site securely and without errors (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). A data processing agreement is in place with Vercel; transfers to the USA are based on the EU-US Data Privacy Framework or on standard contractual clauses.

4. Contact form and enquiries

If you contact us via the form, by email or by phone, we store your details (name, company, email, phone, message) in order to handle your enquiry and contact you. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (pre-contractual measures) or Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in responding). The data is stored in our customer system, which is operated by Supabase (data centre in Frankfurt am Main, Germany). We delete it once the enquiry has been dealt with and no statutory retention obligations apply, at the latest after 24 months without further contact.

5. Online appointment booking

To book a call, we process your name, email, phone number, company, your request and the selected time slot. To prevent double bookings, we check available times against our Google Calendar (Google Ireland Ltd.). The appointment is entered there, and you receive an invitation and confirmation by email. The call takes place by video conference; you will receive the link with the confirmation. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR.

6. Job applications

We use application data (contact details, information provided in the form, CV) exclusively for the application process (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR, Art. 88 GDPR). It is stored in our applicant system (Supabase, Frankfurt) and is only accessible to the persons responsible. If no working relationship is established, we delete the data six months after the process has been completed, unless you have consented to longer storage. We do not select candidates based on age, gender, origin or photo.

7. Recipients

We only pass on your data to service providers who support us in our operations (hosting, database, email and calendar) and who are contractually bound. Your data is not passed on for advertising purposes. Within the GM Group, we only share enquiries if they concern another company of the group (e.g. GM Recruiting or GNT Media).

8. Your rights

You have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and the right to object to processing based on legitimate interests (Art. 15 to 21 GDPR). You may withdraw any consent given at any time with effect for the future. To do so, simply write to info@gmsrvcs.com.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority, for example the Bulgarian Комисия за защита на личните данни (Commission for Personal Data Protection) (cpdp.bg) or the authority at your place of residence.

9. Encryption

The site and all forms are transmitted exclusively in encrypted form via HTTPS.

10. Local storage in the browser

We remember your language choice in your browser's storage (localStorage). This is not a cookie and is not transmitted to us. You can delete it in your browser at any time.

11. Google Maps

On the contact page we show a map from Google Maps (Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland). As soon as the map on the contact page comes into view, it is loaded from Google servers. Google then receives your IP address and technical details about your browser and may set cookies. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in showing our location in a way that is easy to find (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). More in the Google privacy policy.
