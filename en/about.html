GM Services is more than just a management consultancy. We are a growth incubator for established mid-sized B2B companies, building the structures, processes and systems that make growth predictable.

Excellence comes from people

At GM Services, experienced consultants, specialists and partners bring deep expertise in sales, marketing, operations and company building.

Every client works 1:1 with an expert

No group formats, no mass programmes, but tailored strategies and execution, backed by personal support of the highest standard.

Turning good companies into excellent ones

We work exclusively with established B2B service providers where we can create real value. Our focus is on professionalising structures and scaling successful companies: with higher revenue, greater profitability and sustainable growth.

Frequently asked questions

GM Services at a glance

01What does GM Services do?

GM Services is a growth incubator for established mid-sized B2B companies. We combine strategy and execution from a single source and work with companies to build sales, marketing, operations and key account management as a system.

02Where is GM Services based?

GM SERVICES Ltd. is based at ul. Polkovnik Lukashov 5, 3000 Vratsa, Bulgaria. Our teams work in Vratsa and Sofia.

03Who runs GM Services?

GM Services is led by Georgi Trifonov (Founder & CEO) and Mihael Trifonov (Co-Founder).

04Which companies belong to the GM Group?

Alongside GM Services, the GM Group includes GM Recruiting (recruitment), GNT Media (social media and websites) and Gemini (offering for private clients).

05Which companies does GM Services work for?

For established B2B companies that want to grow predictably and need clear structures, processes and systems in sales, marketing and execution to do so.
