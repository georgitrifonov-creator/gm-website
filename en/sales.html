Frequently asked questions

What clients ask us beforehand

01How does the collaboration work in practice?

We start by analysing where you stand today: figures, processes and team. From this we build an implementation plan with clear priorities. Then we put it into practice together, week by week, with fixed meetings, clear tasks and visible KPIs.

02When will we see the first results?

Most clients see the first measurable changes within the first 30 days. After around 90 days, the new structures make a noticeable difference in day-to-day business. Exactly how fast depends on your starting point and the pace of implementation.

03Will we have a dedicated contact person?

Yes. You work 1:1 with an experienced expert from our team who knows your company, your figures and your team. No changing contacts, no group programmes.

04What does working with you cost?

That depends on which areas we build and how much we implement for you. In the free strategy call, you get an honest assessment of what makes sense and the range of investment involved.

05Do we already need our own sales team for this?

No. We start with what you have, even if so far only the management sells. If the team is to grow, we support you with recruiting, onboarding and compensation.
