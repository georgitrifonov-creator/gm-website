In selected locations such as Sofia, Dubai, Monaco, Miami and St. Moritz, we combine strategic expertise, exclusive networks and extraordinary venues into a format with a clear focus on implementation.

The focus is on scaling, leadership, branding, sales and recruiting: interactive workshops, real-world case studies and direct exchange with successful entrepreneurs. Not a motivational event, but a strategic space for decision-makers.

Your host

Georgi Trifonov, entrepreneur and founder of GM Services, specialising in strategic scaling, high-performance sales and positioning. Further speakers will be announced for each event.

  • you see networking as small talk.
  • you want a quick motivation boost rather than long-term excellence.

… but exactly for you if …

  • you don't want to consume, but to implement.
  • you want to take your company structurally to the next level with clear strategies.
  • you are looking for access to entrepreneurs with real results, not just reach.
  • you understand that growth comes from your environment.
  • you are ready to take responsibility for your expansion.
  • you want to combine learning, networking and experience at an international level.
